Each guide takes one goal, assumes you arrived wanting it, and stops when it is met. They
do not teach and they do not justify. Where a step rests on a design decision, the
[explanation](/explanation/) page carrying it is linked, and the exact spellings are in
[reference](/reference/).

If you do not yet have a goal, you want a [tutorial](/tutorials/) instead.

## [Modding](/how-to/modding/)

For players and mod authors, on a working GkPlus install: profiles and mods, replacing
assets, generating maps for the Vulkan renderer, and writing scripts, panels and levels.

## [Development](/how-to/development/)

For developers working on `d3d8.dll` itself: optimized builds, the test suites, mirroring
game structs, subsystems, detours, `gk` bindings, crash dumps, profiling, renderer
comparison and Ghidra.
