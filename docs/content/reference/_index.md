Look things up here. These pages describe what exists (a key, a flag, a function, a field)
and nothing else: no instructions, no reasoning. For a job, use the
[how-to guides](/how-to/); for reasoning, [explanation](/explanation/).

Two of the three sets are **generated from the source** and are regenerated rather than
edited; the commands that regenerate them are on their section pages and collected in
`docs/README.md`.

## [C++ API](/reference/cpp/)

Generated by clang-doc from `src/`, `huffman/`, `imgui-quickjs/` and `utils/`, and browsable
at [`/api/cpp/`](/api/cpp/). The section page carries the build command, the
coverage figures and the four things the generator structurally cannot carry.

- [C++ API reference](/reference/cpp/): the generator, its flags, and what is covered.
- [Namespace map](/reference/cpp/namespaces/): every namespace, the headers that declare
  it, and what it covers.

## [JavaScript API](/reference/javascript/)

Generated by TypeDoc from `types/gk.d.ts` and `types/imgui.d.ts`, and browsable at
[`/api/js/`](/api/js/). This is the surface a mod author writes against: the `"gk"` module's
namespaces, the `ImGui` interface, the entry-module contracts and the level types.
